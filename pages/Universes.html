The Forgotten Village

Description

Although not the first universe the player encounters, The Forgotten Village lies at the base of the interconnected universes, alongside Floor-1: Althem Kingdom.


Once a thriving settlement under the rule of the Althem Kingdom, this village was both built and governed by the kingdom. However, during the outbreak of the Big War-a conflict between the three great nations (Althem Kingdom, Ultarin Kingdom, and The Umi Forces) —the village was abandoned. Its remote location, separated from the main kingdom by the sea, made it too difficult to defend.


Since its abandonment, the original name of the village has been lost to time. It became known simply as The Forgotten Village, a title that reflects the fading memories of its former glory. Only a handful of its original citizens, who lived centuries ago, ever knew its true name, a secret now buried with the past.


Despite its desolation, the village remains a cornerstone of the Althem Kingdom's legacy. It was the birthplace of the Althem Guild, the founders of the kingdom itself. Its towering walls, still standing strong, kept the village safe from invading mobs and creatures of the land.


Legends whisper of hidden secrets buried within the village, secrets capable of granting adventurers unparalleled knowledge and strength—perhaps even unlocking the path to unimaginable power.

Workstations

Upgrade

  • Used to enhance both weapons and armors.

  • To upgrade an item, you must acquire an ore crystal that matches the item's rarity, along with roils, whose cost increases by a fixed amount with each upgrade.

  • Additionally, every item has an upgrade limit determined by its rarity.

Dismantle

  • Allows you to break down an item into its base components—its original ore crystal (of matching rarity) and a fixed amount of roils.

  • For an unupgraded item, dismantling returns one ore crystal.

  • If the item has been upgraded, you receive one additional ore crystal for each upgrade performed, resulting in a total of the number of upgrades plus one.

Crystal Forge

  • Allows you to convert crystals from one rarity to another.

  • By merging multiple crystals of the same rarity, you can obtain a single crystal of a different rarity, enabling you to optimize your resources for further upgrades.

Shop

  • Every Market features a shop that offers items with attributes nearly reaching Universe Level.
